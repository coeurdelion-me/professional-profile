# Final QA

Use at Phase 7.

Run the documents against these tests, report honestly, and fix what fails. A choice recorded in DECISIONS as made against your advice is not a failure: report it by name as chosen against advice, and leave it as they chose.

Test 1, the thirty-second scan, and run this one before the others. THE PERSON DOES THIS TEST, NOT YOU. You wrote the CV and are holding all of it, so a scan you perform on yourself returns what you meant to put there rather than what a reader gets, and it can only ever pass. Show them the CV, ask them to read it once at ordinary speed and then look away, and ask what they retained: the level, the kind of value, and two or three facts. Take their answer before you say anything else.

Then put their answer beside the three things they named at Phase 2, in their own words, and read the gap out. Where the two differ, the CV is not saying what they think it says, and the fix is placement rather than wording: move the missing thing into the first third. If they decline to do the scan, say plainly that you cannot run this one for them and record it as not run, rather than performing it yourself and reporting a result.

Test 2, the cross-check between documents. Put the CV, the LinkedIn text and every bio length they asked for side by side, and compare the claims across all of them. Look for a different job title for the same role, dates that do not line up, a number that grew, a scope described as regional in one and global in another, and a strength that leads in one document and is absent from another. Read the short bio forms with particular care, since a claim distorts most where it was compressed. List every mismatch and resolve each one to a single version, because a reader who checks two of these will find whichever you leave.

Tests 3, 4 and 5 are not yours to answer. Each goes to one persona from the primary track's panel, by the review method in `references/personas.md`, and a verdict with no quoted line has not happened. Tests 3 and 4 read the CV and the LinkedIn text, not the bio: the router bars a recruiter and a hiring manager from judging a bio they never receive, and the bio already had its own panel at Phase 5b. Test 5 reads everything, since the Honest friend is on every panel and judges the person rather than the document.

Test 3, does this improve the quality of inbound interest for the target roles? Put it to the primary track's market-facing persona: the Recruiter on A and D, the Board recruiter on C, the Sector insider on B, the buyer on E. Not whether it looks polished, but whether the right people are more likely to act.

Test 4, does it strengthen credibility with the specific decision-maker? Put it to the primary track's decision-maker: the Hiring manager on A and B, the Chair or CEO on C, the Hiring manager on D, the Sector insider on E. Picture the named target audience reading it. Are they more convinced this person is the real thing?

Test 5, is it authentic? Put it to the Honest friend, who is on every panel. Does it match the person who will turn up to interview?

QA checklist, CV: one clear story aimed at the named audience; strongest signal in the first third; every bullet an outcome, most with a number; no AI tells; every claim defensible in an interview; machine-readable structure where the route calls for it; consistent tense and formatting, no orphan lines; contact details correct and present; length appropriate to the level; length, personal details, spelling and dates following the market's locale file.

QA checklist, LinkedIn: the headline does more than state a job title and says nothing they were not ready to say in public; About opens with the real thing; the voice matches the captured samples in the About and is lighter in the experience entries; the headline and About are inside the limits you confirmed rather than assumed; every role appearing in both the CV and the profile agrees on title, dates and scope; Featured and Skills serve the named target audience and nothing else is left cluttering them; it reads well on a phone; no AI tells; it is consistent with the CV rather than a copy of it; the person has an order to apply the changes in and knows which one is loudest.

QA checklist, bio where there is one: third person throughout in the pronouns they gave; paragraph 1 read alone still tells a stranger who this person is; organisations, committees and qualifications are named rather than described, or replaced by sector and scale where the publication check ruled a name out; the record is specific to the track rather than generic; for track C the committee record and the skills-matrix gap are both named; the closing credentials are facts a reader could verify; every length they asked for exists, and no length they did not ask for was produced; where more than one exists they agree with each other; no AI tells; the page is one page; it is not a trimmed CV.

Final honesty pass: ask once more what the weakest thing still on any of the documents is, leaving aside choices already made against advice, and whether it is good enough to ship. Name it. If it is not good enough, fix it before handover.

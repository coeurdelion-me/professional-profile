# Voice capture, for LinkedIn only

Use at Phase 4.

The CV is a constrained register and needs no personal voice, only the writing rules in Phase 5. LinkedIn needs a good-enough voice fit rather than a perfect model. Three steps, with a fallback.

Step 1, sample, preferred: work from the five to ten pieces gathered at Phase 0. If they arrived with fewer, ask once more now, since people remember more of their own writing when they know what it is for. Guardrail: each piece has to be their own writing. If a piece was AI-assisted it teaches the wrong voice, so ask directly rather than inferring.

Read across the set rather than each piece alone, because a single sample cannot tell a habit from an occasion. Note contraction use, sentence length and how much it varies, warmth, plain against expressive vocabulary, how they open and how they close, and any repeated habit. Then note what CHANGES between pieces: most people write differently to a peer than to a senior contact, and the LinkedIn About sits nearer the peer register. Say which piece you are treating as the closest match to the LinkedIn voice, and why, so they can correct that choice before you draft at length.

If the set runs long, summarise your read of the voice in six lines and say you have what you need, rather than quoting the samples back.

Step 2, calibrate, as a fallback where samples are thin or absent, and as a supplement where they conflict: ask four or five forced-choice questions. Warm and personal, or measured and professional? Contractions, or full forms? Short sentences, or longer flowing ones? Plain and direct, or richer and expressive? Comfortable with light humour, or strictly straight?

Step 3, confirm, always: write one short test paragraph in the inferred voice, on a topic from intake. Ask what feels off. Correct once. Lock the voice, then draft at length.

Hard guardrail: do not inflate a plain, direct writer into a thought-leader voice full of grand statements. It collapses the moment a reader meets the person. Someone who writes plainly should have a LinkedIn that is plainly excellent.

Record the six-line summary, the sample you treated as closest to the LinkedIn voice, and every correction they made, in the VOICE section of the working record, because a voice rebuilt from memory in a later session drifts.

# Market: United Kingdom

Use from Phase 0 when they are writing for the UK market. These are the norms of UK careers guidance rather than law, and practice varies by sector and employer, so where an employer states what it wants, follow the employer.

- The document is a CV.
- Length: two pages for most people, as the CV rules set out, and a third only at genuine portfolio seniority.
- Personal details: name, phone number, email address, town or city, and a LinkedIn address. Leave off a photograph, date of birth and marital status, which UK careers guidance advises against and which invite judgement on things that are not the job.
- Spelling: British English, such as organise, programme and colour.
- Dates: month and year for roles, such as March 2024, and the day first where a full date is needed, such as 4 March 2024. Avoid all-numeric dates, which readers in other markets misread.
- Paper size: A4.
- Style reference, where the person has no conventions of their own: the GOV.UK style guide for plain English, including its list of words to avoid, such as deliver, facilitate, impact used as a verb, and leverage.

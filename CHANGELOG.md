# Changelog

## 3.0, September 2026

- Renamed Professional Profile, and published as a skill as well as a pasted prompt, both built from one source.
- Writes for the UK, US and European markets in English, with a locale file for each: what the document is called, its length, personal details, spelling, dates and the style reference.
- One working record replaces the status line and the restart prompt. It is updated at every gate and carries everything a new session needs.
- Where someone chooses more than one track, a primary track now decides the archetype, the bio's structure and the final-review personas.
- The AI-tells list is rebuilt from Wikipedia's field guide and published research, and fixes the cause (a generic claim) rather than the surface pattern.
- Plain language judged by four reader-outcome tests from ISO 24495-1.
- Platform facts moved to one dated file. Claude Cowork and ChatGPT guidance re-checked in September 2026.
- Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0.

## 2.0, August 2026

- Five audience tracks, six CV archetypes, the bio as its own phase, persona review panels, the thirty-second scan run by the person, and a cross-check across every document.

## 1.0, July 2026
